--- The Bithack Organization ---

Curators of CSEC

The Bithack Organization is a team of developers who has expertise in computer security for the hacking simulation game "Grey Hack". They work on a set of tools for finding and using exploits, aid in writing software for finding exploits, and other tools known as "Bithack Extensions" to keep you safe while hacking.

(c) 2026 The Bithack Organization
The Bithack Organizations sole purpose is to develop tools for the video game Grey Hack. No real world hacking or exploitation is involved.
